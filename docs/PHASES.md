# Daskalo Curriculum Books

This document outlines the strict grammatical progression of the Daskalo application.
It is auto-generated from the per-book YAML files in `shared/data/books/`.
Every chapter generated by the CLI must adhere to the grammar concepts defined here.
Vocabulary is dynamic based on operator topic input, but the underlying structures remain fixed.

## Book 1: The Absolute Basics (Target: A1.1)
**Goal:** Master the building blocks of Greek: the alphabet, core verbs, and your first real sentences.

*   **Chapter 1.1** (ID: `b1_c1`, Length: `short`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. The Greek alphabet: all 24 letters, their names, and their sounds (vowels α, ε, η, ι, ο, υ, ω; consonants β, γ, δ, ζ, θ, κ, λ, μ, ν, ξ, π, ρ, σ/ς, τ, φ, χ, ψ).
        * 2. Stress marks (τόνος): every Greek word of more than one syllable carries exactly one accent mark indicating the stressed syllable.
        * 3. The verb "to be" — είμαι — fully conjugated in the present tense:
        * εγώ είμαι, εσύ είσαι, αυτός/αυτή/αυτό είναι, εμείς είμαστε, εσείς είστε/είσαστε, αυτοί/αυτές/αυτά είναι.
        * 4. Personal subject pronouns: εγώ, εσύ, αυτός, αυτή, αυτό, εμείς, εσείς, αυτοί/αυτές/αυτά.
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** είμαι (I am), εσύ (you), αυτός (he), αυτή (she), εμείς (we), καλημέρα (good morning), γεια (hello), ναι (yes), όχι (no), ευχαριστώ (thank you)
*   **Chapter 1.2** (ID: `b1_c2`, Length: `short`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. Definite articles in the nominative case (subject position):
        * masculine ο (ο άνδρας — the man), feminine η (η γυναίκα — the woman), neuter το (το παιδί — the child).
        * 2. Noun gender: every Greek noun has a grammatical gender (masculine/feminine/neuter) which must agree with its article.
        * 3. The verb "to have" — έχω — fully conjugated in the present tense:
        * εγώ έχω, εσύ έχεις, αυτός/αυτή/αυτό έχει, εμείς έχουμε, εσείς έχετε, αυτοί/αυτές/αυτά έχουν.
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** ο (the — masculine), η (the — feminine), το (the — neuter), έχω (I have), έχει (he/she/it has), παρακαλώ (please), συγγνώμη (excuse me), πολύ (very), λίγο (a little), καλά (well)
*   **Chapter 1.3** (ID: `b1_c3`, Length: `medium`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. Regular present-tense verbs ending in -ω (Type A active voice), first conjugation:

        * Second example: πίνω (to drink) — πίνω, πίνεις, πίνει, πίνουμε, πίνετε, πίνουν.
        * 2. Basic interrogative words: τι (what), πού (where), πώς (how), ποιος/ποια/ποιο (who/which), πότε (when).
        * 3. Simple yes/no questions: intonation-based (same word order as a statement, rising intonation).
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** μιλώ (I speak), πίνω (I drink), τι (what), πού (where), πώς (how), μαθαίνω (I learn), καταλαβαίνω (I understand), γιατί (why), εδώ (here), εκεί (there)

## Book 3: Moving in Time (Target: A2.1)
**Goal:** Break free from the present tense — talk about the future and narrate past events.

*   **Chapter 3.1** (ID: `b3_c1`, Length: `medium`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. The simple future tense with θα:
        * θα + present-tense stem forms the continuous future (what will be happening).
        * θα + aorist stem (perfective stem) forms the simple future (what will happen, completed action).
        * Example: θα πίνω (I will be drinking — continuous) vs θα πιω (I will drink — simple).
        * 2. Future with common regular verbs: γράφω→θα γράψω, διαβάζω→θα διαβάσω, τρώω→θα φάω, πίνω→θα πιω.
        * 3. Time adverbs for the future: αύριο (tomorrow), σύντομα (soon), μετά (later/after), την επόμενη εβδομάδα (next week).
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** θα (future particle), αύριο (tomorrow), σύντομα (soon), μετά (later), θα πάω (I will go), μεθαύριο (the day after tomorrow), ποτέ (never), πάντα (always), το πρωί (in the morning), θα φάω (I will eat)
*   **Chapter 3.2** (ID: `b3_c2`, Length: `medium`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. The simple past (Aorist) of regular -ω verbs (Type A):
        * Stress shifts to the third syllable from the end (or the first syllable in short verbs).
        * Endings: -α, -ες, -ε, -αμε, -ατε, -αν.
        * Example: γράφω → έγραψα (I wrote), αγοράζω → αγόρασα (I bought), διαβάζω → διάβασα (I read).
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** έγραψα (I wrote), αγόρασα (I bought), διάβασα (I read - past), έκανα (I did), πέρυσι (last year), άνοιξα (I opened), έκλεισα (I closed), έφτιαξα (I made/fixed), άρχισα (I started), τελείωσα (I finished)
*   **Chapter 3.3** (ID: `b3_c3`, Length: `medium`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. Syllabic augment in the aorist: verbs of two or fewer syllables take the ε- prefix in the aorist (γράφω → έγραψα, πίνω → ήπια).
        * 2. Negation in the past: δεν + aorist form.
        * Example: Δεν αγόρασα τίποτα. (I didn't buy anything.)
        * 3. Time adverbs for the past: χθες (yesterday), πριν (before/ago), την περασμένη εβδομάδα (last week), πέρυσι (last year).
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** χθες (yesterday), πριν (before/ago), δεν (not — negation), την περασμένη εβδομάδα (last week), ήπια (I drank), δεν ήπια (I didn't drink), δεν έγραψα (I didn't write), πριν από λίγο (a while ago), μόλις (just), τελικά (finally)
*   **Chapter 3.4** (ID: `b3_c4`, Length: `long`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. The simple past (Aorist) of high-frequency irregular verbs:
        * - βλέπω → είδα (I saw): είδα, είδες, είδε, είδαμε, είδατε, είδαν.
        * - λέω → είπα (I said): είπα, είπες, είπε, είπαμε, είπατε, είπαν.
        * - πηγαίνω/πάω → πήγα (I went): πήγα, πήγες, πήγε, πήγαμε, πήγατε, πήγαν.
        * - τρώω → έφαγα (I ate): έφαγα, έφαγες, έφαγε, φάγαμε, φάγατε, έφαγαν.
        * - έρχομαι → ήρθα (I came): ήρθα, ήρθες, ήρθε, ήρθαμε, ήρθατε, ήρθαν.
        * 2. Mixing regular and irregular aorist in the same narrative to tell a coherent short story in the past.
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** είδα (I saw), είπα (I said), πήγα (I went), έφαγα (I ate), ήρθα (I came), βρήκα (I found), έμεινα (I stayed), άκουσα (I heard), κατάλαβα (I understood), ήπια (I drank)
*   **Chapter 3.5** (ID: `b3_c5`, Length: `medium`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. A second batch of high-frequency irregular aorist verbs.
        * 2. παίρνω → πήρα, δίνω → έδωσα, βάζω → έβαλα, βγάζω → έβγαλα, μαθαίνω → έμαθα.
        * 3. Building fluency by combining all aorist verbs in short narratives about giving, receiving, and learning.
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** πήρα (I took), έδωσα (I gave), έβαλα (I put), έβγαλα (I took out), έμαθα (I learned), άφησα (I left/let), έστειλα (I sent), έχασα (I lost), βρήκα (I found), άλλαξα (I changed)
*   **Chapter 3.6** (ID: `b3_c6`, Length: `short`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. The verb μπορώ (can / to be able to) fully conjugated in the present tense:
        * 2. μπορώ, μπορείς, μπορεί, μπορούμε, μπορείτε, μπορούν.
        * 3. Aorist of μπορώ: μπόρεσα (I managed / I was able to).
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** μπορώ (I can), μπορείς (you can), μπορούμε (we can), μπόρεσα (I managed), δεν μπορώ (I cannot), μπορείς να με βοηθήσεις; (can you help me?), μπορώ να μπω; (may I come in?), βοήθεια (help), ερώτηση (question), άδεια (permission/leave)
*   **Chapter 3.7** (ID: `b3_c7`, Length: `medium`)

        * 1. Using μπορώ + να + subjunctive (aorist stem) to express ability or permission:
        * Μπορώ να φύγω; (Can I leave? / May I leave?)
        * Μπορείς να έρθεις αύριο; (Can you come tomorrow?)
        * 2. Using πρέπει + να + subjunctive (aorist stem) to express necessity:
        * Πρέπει να φύγω τώρα. (I have to leave now.)
        * Δεν πρέπει να αργήσεις. (You mustn't be late.)
        * 3. Using θέλω + να + subjunctive in past contexts with compound structures.
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** πρέπει (must/have to), θέλω να (I want to), μπορώ να (I can/may), δεν πρέπει (mustn't), ίσως (maybe), ελπίζω (I hope), χρειάζεται (it is needed), χρειάζομαι (I need), αναγκαίο (necessary), φαίνεται (it seems)

        * Key signal words: συνήθως (usually), πάντα (always), κάθε μέρα (every day), τότε (back then).

    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** ενώ (while), όταν (when), εκείνη τη στιγμή (at that moment), ξαφνικά (suddenly), παράλληλα (at the same time), μόλις (as soon as), αμέσως (immediately), μετά από αυτό (after that), πριν από αυτό (before that), τελικά (in the end)

        * 3. Agreement of εαυτός with the subject in gender, number, and case.

## Book 5: Nuance and Subtlety (Target: B1.2)
**Goal:** Express possibilities, use the perfect tenses, and enrich your sentences with prepositions and pronouns.

*   **Chapter 5.1** (ID: `b5_c1`, Length: `medium`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. The Present Perfect (Παρακείμενος): Formed with έχω + the invariable aorist infinitive.
        * Example: έχω γράψει (I have written), έχεις δει (you have seen), έχει φάει (he has eaten).
        * Used for actions completed in the past with results relevant to the present.
        * Time markers: ήδη (already), ακόμα (yet/still), μόλις (just), ποτέ (never — with perfect).
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** έχω κάνει (I have done), έχω φάει (I have eaten), έχω δει (I have seen), ήδη (already), ακόμα (yet/still), μόλις (just now), ποτέ (never — with perfect), μέχρι τώρα (until now), πρόσφατα (recently), αρκετές φορές (several times)
*   **Chapter 5.2** (ID: `b5_c2`, Length: `short`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. The Past Perfect (Υπερσυντέλικος): Formed with είχα + aorist infinitive.
        * Example: είχα γράψει (I had written), είχες δει (you had seen).
        * Used for an action completed before another past action.
        * Example: Όταν ήρθες, είχα ήδη φύγει. (When you arrived, I had already left.)
        * 2. Contrast between present perfect and past perfect in context.

    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** ρώτησα (I asked), απάντησα (I answered), αν (if/whether), εξήγησα (I explained), αναρωτιέμαι (I wonder), δεν ξέρω αν (I don't know if), δεν ξέρω πού (I don't know where), προσπάθεια (effort), απορία (question/doubt), κατανόηση (understanding)
*   **Chapter 8.6** (ID: `b8_c6`, Length: `medium`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. Passive voice of Type B verbs (ending in -ώ) — present tense:
        * Pattern: -ιέμαι, -ιέσαι, -ιέται, -ιόμαστε, -ιέστε, -ιούνται.
        * Examples: αγαπιέμαι (I am loved), γεννιέμαι (I am born), εξηγούμαι (I am explained), κρατιέμαι (I hold myself).
        * 2. Aorist of Type B passive verbs: -ήθηκα ending.
        * Examples: γεννήθηκα (I was born), αγαπήθηκα (I was loved), ενημερώθηκα (I was informed).
        * 3. Compare with Type A passive (-ομαι) forms to highlight the pattern difference.
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** αγαπιέμαι (I am loved), γεννιέμαι (I am born), γεννήθηκα (I was born), εξηγούμαι (I am explained), κρατιέμαι (I hold myself back), ενημερώθηκα (I was informed), αγαπήθηκα (I was loved), εμπειρία (experience), γνώμη (opinion), σκέψη (thought)
*   **Chapter 8.7** (ID: `b8_c7`, Length: `short`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. Impersonal passive expressions — third-person singular passive for general statements:

## Book 9: Fluency and Complexity (Target: C1.2)
**Goal:** Express hypothetical pasts, connect complex arguments, and use idiomatic Greek.

*   **Chapter 9.1** (ID: `b9_c1`, Length: `medium`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. Type 3 Conditionals (Unreal/Impossible past):
        * Expressing situations that did not happen in the past and their hypothetical results.
        * Structure: Αν + είχα + aorist infinitive (Past Perfect), θα + είχα + aorist infinitive.
        * Example: Αν είχα διαβάσει, θα είχα περάσει τις εξετάσεις. (If I had studied, I would have passed the exams.)
        * 2. All 6 persons of the Past Perfect (Υπερσυντέλικος): είχα, είχες, είχε, είχαμε, είχατε, είχαν + aorist infinitive.
        * 3. Negation: Αν δεν είχα ..., θα είχα ...
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** θα είχα (I would have), αν είχα (if I had), εξετάσεις (exams), ευκαιρία (opportunity), απόφαση (decision), έχανα (I was missing/losing — imperfect), κέρδιζα (I was winning — imperfect), μετανιώνω (I regret), αν δεν είχα (if I hadn't), θα είχες (you would have)
*   **Chapter 9.2** (ID: `b9_c2`, Length: `short`)
    *   **Grammar Concepts:**
        * 1. Mixed Conditionals — combining Type 2 and Type 3:
        * Past condition with present result: Αν + Past Perfect, θα + Imperfect/Present.
        * Example: Αν είχα σπουδάσει ιατρική, τώρα θα δούλευα ως γιατρός. (If I had studied medicine, I would be working as a doctor now.)
        * 2. Present condition with past result: Αν + Imperfect, θα + Past Perfect.
        * Example: Αν ήμουν πλούσιος, θα είχα αγοράσει εκείνο το σπίτι. (If I were rich, I would have bought that house.)
        * 3. Recognising which tense goes in which clause.
    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** τώρα θα (now would), αλλά τώρα (but now), εκείνη την εποχή (at that time), αν ήμουν (if I were), αν είχα σπουδάσει (if I had studied), αποτέλεσμα (result), επιλογή (choice), μοίρα (fate/destiny), πράγματι (indeed), υποθετικά (hypothetically)
*   **Chapter 9.3** (ID: `b9_c3`, Length: `medium`)

    *   **Mandatory Vocabulary:** ωστόσο (however), μολονότι (although), παρόλο που (even though), επομένως (therefore), συνεπώς (consequently), εντούτοις (nevertheless — formal), κατά συνέπεια (as a consequence), αντίθετα (on the contrary), εξάλλου (besides/moreover), πάντως (anyway/however)
